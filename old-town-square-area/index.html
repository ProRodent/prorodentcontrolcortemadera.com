Why the Old Town Square area is different

  • Older mixed-use buildings

    A blend of homes and small commercial buildings with aging construction.

  • Foot traffic and food waste

    Small shops and cafes nearby draw rodents toward loading and storage areas.

  • Established landscaping

    Older street trees and gardens near the square give rodents cover close to buildings.

The area around Corte Madera's original town square, laid out in 1916 and anchored by Menke Park, holds some of the town's oldest buildings, a mix of homes and small commercial spaces that have stood for generations. That age brings the kind of settled framing, aging trim and older foundation work that gives mice and rats more entry points than a newer part of town typically has.

Small shops, cafes and offices in this area also mean loading areas, trash storage and food waste that draw rodents toward buildings the way any older commercial strip does, even on a modest scale. Homes tucked among these older commercial buildings can see spillover activity from a neighboring business as easily as from their own construction.

What we typically find near the square.

  • Mice and rats entering older buildings through settled foundation and trim gaps.
  • Activity near small commercial trash and storage areas close to Menke Park.
  • Rodents moving between adjacent older buildings through shared walls or narrow gaps.
  • Outdoor activity in established gardens and street tree plantings near the square.

Treating a mixed residential and commercial block.

A property near the square sometimes needs both a residential-style inspection and an awareness of what neighboring commercial spaces are dealing with, since activity does not respect property lines in a tightly built older block. We check shared walls and narrow side-yard gaps specifically in this area, since that is a more common entry route here than in newer, more spaced-out parts of Corte Madera.

If you run a small business near the square rather than a home, our commercial rodent control service covers the documentation and monitoring schedule that a shop or cafe typically needs, applied with the same attention to this neighborhood's older construction.

Sealing older, historic construction.

Sealing work on a building from this era sometimes needs to account for construction details, like original wood trim or masonry foundations, that a modern building would not have. We choose materials and methods that close the gap effectively without unnecessary damage to older finishes, and our rodent exclusion service applies that approach on every visit in this part of town.

Working with property owners and tenants together.

Many buildings near the square have a residential unit above or behind a small commercial space, which means a landlord and a tenant sometimes both need to be involved in scheduling a visit. We coordinate access with whoever is available and keep both parties informed of what was found, since activity in a shared building affects everyone using it.

This area combines some of Corte Madera's oldest construction with a steady flow of foot traffic and deliveries. We treat calls here as a priority for a thorough exterior check, since a missed gap in an older shared wall tends to resurface faster than in a more isolated, newer home.

Parking is often tight near the square, which means yard and side-alley access for a technician needs a bit more planning than a typical residential call. Mentioning where a vehicle can park, or whether foot access from the street is easier, helps the visit start on time.

We leave a short written summary after each visit near the square noting what was found, which side of an older shared wall needed attention, and any recommended follow-up. That documentation is especially useful in a mixed residential and commercial block where more than one party may need to see it.

A quick note on timing: early morning visits before shops open tend to work best near the square, since that avoids the busiest delivery and foot-traffic windows and gives clearer access to shared alleys and side yards.

Questions about Old Town Square Area

Why do older buildings near the square see more activity?

Decades of settling opens small gaps around foundations, trim and old plumbing chases that a newer building simply does not have yet.

Can a neighboring shop's rodent problem affect my home?

Yes, in a tightly built older block, activity can move between adjoining buildings through shared walls or narrow gaps, so nearby commercial spaces matter even for a home.

Do you work with small businesses close to Menke Park?

Yes, our commercial service covers shops, cafes and offices in this area with the documentation many businesses need for inspections or lease terms.

Will sealing work damage older architectural details?

We choose materials and methods that close entry points effectively while minimizing impact on original trim, masonry or other older finishes.
