Make the call count.

A faster, more accurate visit usually comes down to a handful of details ready before you dial: the address and ZIP code, roughly when the activity started, and whether anyone has spotted droppings or a nest already. Mention pets or kids around the affected area too. None of it is required to get help, but it does shorten the call.

Before a technician gets there.

  • Keep kids and pets clear of any spot with droppings, a nest, or a likely entry point.
  • Skip the vacuum or broom on dry droppings, since disturbing them can send contamination into the air.
  • Hold off on setting your own bait or poison until the property has actually been inspected.
  • Wash up after touching anything near a suspected rodent area, gloves or not.
