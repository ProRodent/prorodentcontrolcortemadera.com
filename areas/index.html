Coverage overview

Every town below is matched to its own ZIP code or codes from our coverage data, along with an honest description of its actual jurisdiction. Several of the communities near Corte Madera, including San Quentin, Stinson Beach, Woodacre, Bolinas, San Geronimo, Forest Knolls, Lagunitas, Nicasio and El Sobrante, are unincorporated, meaning they have no separate city hall or mayor and are governed as part of their county instead. The rest are genuinely incorporated towns or cities with their own municipal government, from Larkspur next door to major cities like San Francisco and Oakland further out.

Eleven of the 28 towns on this list sit across San Francisco Bay, reachable only by crossing the Richmond-San Rafael Bridge or the Golden Gate Bridge, not by a short local drive. We still cover them, since they fall within our straight-line coverage radius and the LeadSmart coverage data for this list, but we describe the crossing honestly on each of those pages rather than implying they are just down the road.

San Francisco, Berkeley and Oakland each span many ZIP codes on their own. Rather than listing every ZIP for those cities, we show the five closest verified ZIPs to Corte Madera on each page and confirm your specific ZIP by phone.

If your city is not listed, call anyway. We can usually confirm coverage on the spot, and every visit still starts with the same rodent inspection whether you are a mile from Corte Madera or closer to fifteen.

Distances below are approximate straight-line miles from Corte Madera, not drive time, and are longer in practice for the towns across the bay. Pick your town from the list, or use the map to find the closest match.

Basic safety steps while you wait for a visit.

  • Keep children and pets away from any area with droppings, a nest, or a suspected entry point.
  • Do not sweep or vacuum dry droppings; disturbing them dry can spread contamination into the air.
  • Do not set out your own loose bait or poison before a technician has assessed the property.
  • Wash your hands after touching anything near a suspected rodent area, even if you wore gloves.
